Transcribed from DMBA's 2027 open enrollment slides (medical, prescription, HSA, FSA, vision, and dental). Enrollment deadline is November 13, 2026.

## Medical plans (in-network)

|                                           | PPO 80         | HSA 80         | HSA 60         |
| ----------------------------------------- | -------------- | -------------- | -------------- |
| Coinsurance                               | 20%            | 20%            | 40%            |
| Deductible (single/family)                | $500/$1,000    | $2,500/$5,000  | $4,000/$8,000  |
| Out-of-pocket maximum (single/family)     | $6,000/$10,000 | $7,500/$12,000 | $8,700/$14,500 |
| Employer HSA contribution (single/family) | -              | $625/$1,250    | $625/$1,250    |
| Monthly premium - Single                  | $131.80        | $119.30        | $62.50         |
| Monthly premium - Two-Party               | $269.30        | $243.50        | $109.10        |
| Monthly premium - Family                  | $420.60        | $313.90        | $129.10        |

Enrollment in medical benefits also includes Disability and Occupational Accidental Death & Dismemberment benefits, which have separate premium amounts.

## Copayment/coinsurance per in-network visit

| Service                                                     | PPO 80 | HSA 80 | HSA 60 |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------ | ------ | ------ |
| PCP office visit                                            | $25\*  | 20%\*  | 40%\*  |
| Specialist office visit                                     | $50\*  | 20%\*  | 40%\*  |
| Urgent care                                                 | $60\*  | 20%\*  | 40%\*  |
| Emergency room service                                      | $200\* | 20%\*  | 40%\*  |
| Physical, occupational, and speech therapy, or chiropractic | $50\*  | 20%\*  | 40%\*  |

\* Coverage after deductible is met.

## Prescriptions

Prescription benefits are included in the medical plan and apply to any deductibles and out-of-pocket maximums.

|                                 | PPO 80 | HSA 80 | HSA 60 |
| ------------------------------- | ------ | ------ | ------ |
| Retail 30-day: Tier 1 drugs     | $10\*  | $10    | $15    |
| Retail 90-day: Tier 1 drugs     | $25\*  | $25    | $40    |
| Mail order 90-day: Tier 1 drugs | $20\*  | $20    | $30    |

\* For PPO 80, deductible does not apply to tier 1 drugs. Tiers 2, 3, and specialty: contact Navitus.

## Flexible Spending Accounts

|                     | 2026   | 2027   |
| ------------------- | ------ | ------ |
| Healthcare FSA      | $3,400 | $3,500 |
| Limited Purpose FSA | $3,400 | $3,500 |
| Dependent Care FSA  | $7,500 | $7,500 |

Healthcare FSA (medical, dental, vision, Rx) is for the PPO 80 only; Limited Purpose FSA (dental and vision) is for the HSA plans only. Full annual election is available right away. Must enroll during open enrollment.

## Vision (new for 2027)

Routine eye exams are no longer covered by the medical plans. The two VSP options combine into one plan covering routine exams and eyewear.

| Benefit          | Cost                                          |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Routine eye exam | $10 copayment                                 |
| Frames           | $25 copayment, $150 allowance                 |
| Contact lenses   | $150 allowance                                |
| Contact fitting  | Up to $40 copayment                           |
| Monthly premium  | $7.40 single, $14.30 two-party, $22.00 family |
